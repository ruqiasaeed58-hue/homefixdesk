HomeFixDesk gives clear, practical answers to everyday home repair questions: why something is happening, and how to fix it step by step.

## Who writes the guides

HomeFixDesk is published by a small editorial team of home-maintenance writers. Every guide is written, reviewed, and updated by our editors — articles are not copied from forums, not spun from other sites, and not published without a human review pass.

## How we write a guide

1. **Start with the real question.** We pick topics from the questions homeowners actually ask — the long, specific searches people type when something in their house breaks.
2. **Research before writing.** For each guide we consult manufacturer manuals, building-code basics, and licensed-trade references so the steps match how the job is actually done.
3. **Safety first.** Every guide opens with the hazards involved. Where a job touches electricity, gas, structural work, or water near wiring, we say so plainly and recommend calling a licensed professional instead of doing it yourself.
4. **Review and update.** Published guides are re-checked on a rolling basis. When readers or new product manuals surface something better or safer, the guide is updated and the "Updated" date on the page changes.

## Corrections

We read every message sent to [our contact page](/contact/). If a guide has a wrong step, an outdated product reference, or an unsafe suggestion, we fix it promptly and note the update. Accuracy matters more than volume here.

## Advertising

HomeFixDesk is reader-supported and ad-supported. Display advertising (Google AdSense) helps keep every guide free to read. Ads never influence what we recommend — and we never let advertisers write, edit, or approve our content.
